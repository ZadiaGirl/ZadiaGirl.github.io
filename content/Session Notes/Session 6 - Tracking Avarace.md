The party returned to Hallstat from [[Остров (Ostrov)|Ostrov]] to meet the Demonologist [[Xenni Delotta]] to have her investigate the strange items recovered in [[Avarice|Avarice's]] hideout. Upon returning to Hallstat the party was immediately greeted by the city guard, and was taken into questioning for the fire they had started in the previous session. The party lied to the captain of the guard, saying it was the work of the _"shape shifting demon"_. The captain of the guard has an expert demonologist come in to corroborate the parties story. The expert confirms their story. After being released, she reveals that she is Xenni Delotta, and was there the night of the fire. She calls the party out for their lies, but tells them that if the work together she will keep their secret. Additionally, they find out that the captain of the guard is actually her father, [[Xavier Delotta]]. The party learned about the [[Pact Scroll]], and how the only other recorded existence of the a pact scroll was from [[The Great Calamity]], when a cult leader, [[Ichabod Dyancy]], had formed a pact with [[Lodno]]. 
They find out that the demon lord [[Marianti]] is presumed dead, because no one has seen their spawn in decades. While talking to Xenni, [[Demii (Debbie)|Demii]] see's a strange demon horn that was discovered in [[Kodinsk]], and feels a strange connection to it. After leaving the strange [[Blue Crystal Orb]] with Xenni to do further research on, the party heads out to [[Brimsdale]]. On their way, the are ambushed by Avarice. In the heat of the Battle, [[Steve (Jarod)|Steve]], [[Samson (Austin)|Samson]], and [[Scipio (Henry)|Scipio]] have their consciousness sent to the [[Void Scape|void scape]]. While lost in the void scape, Daxter and Demii were left to fight Avarice on their own. In a moment of brutality, Demii was killed by Avarice.  In the void scape, Steve heard the voice of [[Agon]], where he then kicks Steve's consciousness out of the void scape because it was "too dangerous to let him stay." Daxter tries to heal Demii, but nothing he does seems to help, and notices that their blood has turned purple. Suddenly, something has awoken in Demii's blood, stirred by the [[Purple Demon Dagger]] Avarice used to kill her. Avarice flees, vowing to return to reclaim his scroll. The party decides to return to Xenni, bringing the body of two imps that they killed in the battle. She determines that the imps used to be human, transformed by the blight and some unknown magic. The process isn't perfected yet, having deuterated the body. She warns that once the process is perfected, it could create far more powerful imps. While this is going on, Demii feels a strong pull to the strange horn she saw earlier. When she touches it she see's vivid flashes of her mother and father, who she previously had no memory of. Watching the death of her mother, and realizing that this horn was in fact hers. Not telling the rest of the party of this revelation, she deals with the sudden weight of this realization. Finally, Xenni reveals that the inhibitor chip that was in Steve was also acting as a beacon, allowing Agon and Avarice to track them. After destroying the chip, the party starts off on their journey to Brimsdale once more.
