Upon arriving in [[../../Locations/Ihstar/Brimsdale/Brimsdale|Brimsdale]], the party begins their search for [[../Villain/Avarice|Avarice's]] secret hideout. Starting with the Demon Hunter's guild, the party learns of another demon incursion in the mines, along with the current absence of the guild master, [[../../NPC/Ishtar/Brimsdale/Samire]]. Not gaining any relevant information for their search, the party makes their way to scour the taverns for information. On their way, [[../../Characters/Scipio (Henry)|Scipio]]  feels a strange calling to an old church off the main road. As Scipio enters he finds himself in the presence of [[../../NPC/Ishtar/Brimsdale/Astrian Etherbolt|Astrian Etherbolt]], the keeper of the _monastery of the matron_ in Brimsdale. Through speaking with Astrian, Scipio discovers more about the mysterious [[Nyphriana,|Nyphriana,]]. According to Astrian, the matron chooses a _chosen protector_ in times of great danger. The last chosen one was a warrior named [[../../History/People/Nashton Orvasha, Protector of the Matron|Nashton]], who was chosen before [[../../History/The Great Calamity|The Great Calamity]]. Nashton fought against [[../../History/People/Ichabod Dyancy|Ichabod Dyancy]], and was the one to finally slay him. After learning more about the history of the matron, Astrian gives Scipio the [[../../Items/Relics of the Matron/Breastplate of the Matron|Breastplate of the Matron]], one of the relics of the matron previously used by Nashton.

While Scipio was busy in the monastery, the rest of the gang investigated the nearby cafe, _The Salted Dragon_, where the waitress informs them of a strange cloaked figure with blond hair, and a strange tattoo resembling [[../Villain/Avarice|Avarice's]]. Moving on from the cafe, the party searches _The Ivory Palace_. Learning nothing relevant for their investigation, that party makes their way to [[../../Locations/Ihstar/Brimsdale/The Shack|The Shack]], where they meet [[../../NPC/Ishtar/Brimsdale/Sloan|Sloan]], the leader of the criminal network in Brimsdale. He tells the party that their fence, [[../../NPC/Ishtar/Brimsdale/Vetements Homme|Monsieur Vetements]] has information pertaining to their investigation, but needs them to "send a message" for him. Turns out Vetements was doing deals that violated the agreement made with Sloan. The party discovers that he sold large amount of Demonite, candles, and an exotic animal to the blond haired man with the hand tattoo, and delivered it to the basement of _The Ivory Palace_. 

After gaining all the information they could, the party set off to the mines to clear the demons and collect the reward from the hunters guild. While in the mines the party discovered strange runes. Recognizing the runes as ones drawn in the book at the monastery, Scipio leads the party back to Astrian. Astrian tells them that they are runes used by Ichabod, and directs the party to meet with [[../../NPC/Ishtar/Brimsdale/Dr. Prior|Dr. Prior]] for more information. Meeting with Dr. Prior, the party learns that these runes are binding runes, used to link disciples are bound to their leader through these runes. They are basically minor contracts, allowing someone contracted to a demon lord to _lend_ a small portion of their power to their disciples in order for them to carry out the leaders bidding; but also ensures the disciples loyalty to the leader. Showing Dr. Prior a drawing of Avarice, he is able to identify the tattoos as runic tattoos, Furthermore, Dr. Prior is able to determine that [[../../Characters/Pets/Jak|Jak]] used to be a normal rat, but seems to have a mutated form of demon blood mixed within his own. Making him fireproof, and maybe more? 

The party, armed with this knowledge, goes to collect their reward from the demon hunters guild, does a little shopping, before finally heading off to met [[../../NPC/Ishtar/Brimsdale/Maurice|Maurice]], a tech master that has made quite a lot of commotion for his explosive studies. On their way to the tech masters shop, Steve begins to feel a strange static come over him, making him dizzy and disoriented. Leading to a series of malfunctions and spasms. Meeting with Maurice reveals that the removal of the inhibitor chip damaged a core part of his synaptic circuits. Maurice was able to give Steve a temporary fix, but this has left Steve particularly fragile. Knowing that no human has the knowledge to fully restore his circuits to full function, Steve is left to find a way to convince a demon to help fix him.
