The party took the only job on the board, to deal with demon worms in [[Ohlsdorf]]. However, they are stopped by the city guard, informed that a demon is on a rampage and everyone is looking for it. It turns out that this "demon" is actually, [[Avarice]]. A demon hunter from [[Остров (Ostrov)|Ostrov]]. Avarice manages to escape, leaving the party to investigate his hide out; discovering a magically sealed scroll, and a strange blue crystal in a metal cage type thing. [[Steve (Jarod)|Steve's]] system is malfunctioning for some strange, unknown reason, unable to tell that these items are magical. Even though they clearly are. Inside the hideout they also found a [[Fire Dagger]], a body with it's heart removed, and a map, leading them to the home town of Avarice, in Ostrov. They set fire to the hideout and set off to Ostrov, where the demon hunters guild master, [[Dyancy Billsworth]], tells them of the tragic death of Avarice's family, and of the the whereabouts of his old demon hunting partner, [[Josipin]]. Josipin informs the party that Avarice has been working to kill [[Lodno]], and had discovered something that he believed would allow him to finally do so. Shortly after is when [[Kalt]] was sent to kill both him and his family. He seemed unsurprised to hear of what had become of Avarice, and gave the party information about a stash house in [[Brimsdale]]. The party also went to a constructs expert, [[Nora]], who discovered the source of the interference in Steve's processing. She removed an inhibitor chip, finally allowing Steve to properly function.
