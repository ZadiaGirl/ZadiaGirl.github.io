A magical needle, created to infuse demon magic into the flesh of living creatures. The needle can be used to inscribe a tattoo onto a creature. The tattoo contains the magical essence of a spell. The tattoo, when activated, will cast the affects of the spell. It is an ancient artifact, once owned by the old king of Ishtar before the fall of the kingdom. When the kingdom fell and the republic took over, the needle was lost to [[Agon]] in the midst of a giant demon invasion.
