Med Packs are used to heal the player, the amount they heal is dependent on the level of the med pack. They can be taken as a bonus action, or given to another as an action. The table below shows the amount of health recovered.

| Lv  | HP    |
| --- | ----- |
| 1   | 4d4+4 |
| 2   | 4d6+6 |
| 3   | 4d8+8 |
