#Mechanic
Corruption is the level of mental and physical decay caused by the use of [[Mods#Complex|complex mods]]. 
# Level 1
- -1 to ability checks

# Level 2
- -1 to saving throws

# Level 3
- -5 to your max HP

# Level 4
- disadvantage on ability checks

# Level 5
- disadvantage on saving throws

# Level 6
- -10 to max HP
