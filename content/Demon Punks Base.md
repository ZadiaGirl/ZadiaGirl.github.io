The base is located in the slums of [[Locations/Ihstar/Hallstat|Hallstat]]. 

# Base Upgrades
-  Priming station (400gp): 
	- Lv 1: Increase your weapon by the +1
	- Lv 2: Increase the damage bonus to 1d4
	- Lv 3 (1000gp): increase the damage to 1d6 per Lv past 3. 
- Medical Bay (1,000gp): 
	- Lv 1: You build a station to modify your mods. Additionally you can synthesize 1 med pack per day. Spend $25 to synthesize another med pack. Med packs heal 4d4+4 HP when used and can be taken as a bonus action. Or applied to another as an action. 
	- Lv 2: You get 1x(Player Count) [[Items/Med Packs]] for free. Cost decreases to $15. Med Packs heal 4d6+6 HP.
	- Lv 3: You get 3x(Player Count) [[Items/Med Packs]]. Med Packs heal 4d8+6 HP.
- Bank (500gp):
	- Lv 1: You can deposit gold into the bank. You gain 5% interest after the money has been deposited for 3 sessions.   
	- Lv 2 (1,000gp): 10% Interest.
	- Lv 3 (1,500gp): 20%
- [[Demon Research Bay]] (750gp):
	- Lv 1: You can extract simple parts and materials from demons. These can be used to create items, mods, and weapons.
